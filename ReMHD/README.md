This is a separate study to the Mach number study.
Add resistivity and viscosity to evaluate resistive MHD. This is a more realistic simulation for BNS merger event.
Our new parameter of interest is the magnetic Prandtl number Pm, which gives the ratio between kinematic viscosity and resistivity of fluid or plasma.

Finding the suitable initial shear layer amplitude to ensure our fluid variables were resolved by our numerical resolution was crucial. Hence, we have a test folder and the folder for final setup.
